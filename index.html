About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes.

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he came from a humble background and worked hard to achieve his dreams. His dedication, knowledge and simple personality made him an inspiration to millions of people.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was also a great teacher and motivational speaker who encouraged young people to dream big and work towards building a better nation.

Journey

Key Achievements

1931

Born in Rameswaram

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960

Joined DRDO

Kalam started his professional career as a scientist at the Defence Research and Development Organisation.

1969

Joined ISRO

He joined the Indian Space Research Organisation and contributed to India's space programme.

1980

SLV-III Mission

He played a key role in the successful development of India's Satellite Launch Vehicle programme.

2002

President of India

Dr. Kalam became the 11th President of India and served the nation from 2002 to 2007.

2015

Passed Away

Dr. Kalam passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.
